From bean
to cup.

Four steps, no shortcuts. This is what happens in the days before your coffee reaches the counter.

Source

Small farms we know by name. Paid properly, visited often, and cupped twice before we commit to a single sack.

Origin Huila, Colombia · 1,750 masl

02

Roast

Every Tuesday, in small batches on a 15 kg drum. Light enough to keep the fruit, long enough to find the sweetness.

Roast 15 kg drum · 11 min · First crack at 196°C

03

Grind

Ground to order, never ahead. The burrs are set to the morning's humidity and dialled in again at noon.

Grind 64 mm flat burrs · 18 g dose · 28 s

04

Pour

Water at 94°, poured slow and steady. Thirty seconds of patience, then a cup that tastes like where it came from.

Brew 1:16 ratio · 94°C · 3 min 30 s
