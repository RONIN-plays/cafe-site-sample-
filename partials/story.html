01 — Our story

Est. 2019 / Hollis Lane

We started Marlow with one rule: nothing rushed. Beans roasted on Tuesdays, dough left to rise overnight, and a room where the morning can take as long as it needs. Pull up a chair. Stay for a second cup. The kettle is always warm.

A small room on Hollis Lane, a two-group machine and an oven that runs hot by six. Everything on the counter was made here, by people who would rather do it slowly than do it twice.
